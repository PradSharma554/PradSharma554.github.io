Featured project

CloudAIr — the access layer between websites and AI agents

Websites are increasingly read by AI agents, but site owners can't serve, observe, or control that traffic. CloudAIr crawls a site, auto-generates its llms.txt with Gemini, mirrors every page as clean markdown, and exposes the content as a per-site MCP server — with agent identification, per-agent rate limiting, and an analytics dashboard. TypeScript/Express, Next.js, Postgres; runs entirely on free-tier infrastructure.
